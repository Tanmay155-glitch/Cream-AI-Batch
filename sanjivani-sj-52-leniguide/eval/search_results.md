### Limitations of Semantic Search

- Embedding models can conflate unrelated caves that share similar architectural vocabulary.
- Exact numeric lookups (e.g., *"Cave 32"*) work better with keyword matching.
- Dense embeddings benefit from fine-tuning on archaeological domain terminology.
