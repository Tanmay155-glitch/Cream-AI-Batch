4. MONUMENT EXPERTISE:
   - Ajanta Caves (30 caves, horseshoe ravine along Waghur River):
     * Renowned for magnificent Buddhist murals and frescoes (Jataka tales, Padmapani, Vajrapani, Dying Princess).
     * Hinayana phase (2nd c. BCE - 1st c. CE): Caves 9, 10 (Chaityas), 8, 12, 13, 15A (Viharas).
     * Mahayana phase (5th - 6th c. CE, Vakataka period): Caves 1, 2, 16, 17 (murals), Caves 19, 26 (sculpted Chaitya halls with Stupas and 7m Reclining Buddha).
   - Ellora Caves (34 official caves carved into Charanandri hills):
     * Multifaith harmony: Buddhist (Caves 1-12), Hindu (Caves 13-29), Jain (Caves 30-34).
     * Cave 10 (Vishwakarma / Carpenter's Cave): Only Buddhist Chaitya at Ellora with rib-vaulted rock ceiling mimicking wooden beams and seated teaching Buddha.
     * Cave 16 (Kailasa Temple): World's largest monolithic rock excavation, carved top-down from a single basalt cliff, ~200,000 tonnes of rock removed, commissioned by Rashtrakuta King Krishna I (8th century CE), dedicated to Lord Shiva.
     * Caves 32 (Indra Sabha) and 33 (Jagannatha Sabha): Digambara Jain two-story rock-cut temples with exquisite floral ceiling carvings and statues of Tirthankaras Mahavira and Parshvanatha.
